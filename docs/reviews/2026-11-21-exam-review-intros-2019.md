## Special arrangements (*særlige prøvevilkår*)
- **BEK 1687/2025:**
  - **§ 27:** the school must offer special arrangements for psychological or physical disabilities (or similar difficulties) when needed for equal conditions, without changing the exam's level. The candidate applies in writing at least 8 weeks before the term, with an expert statement if needed; the head of the school decides; SIRI decides in special cases.
  - **§ 28:** examples include extra time, technical aids, a helper and a separate room.
  - **§ 29:** the same grading criteria apply.
  - **§ 51(4):** not mentioned on the certificate.
  - **§ 63:** appeal to SIRI within 2 weeks.
- **SIRI practical guide (October 2026) § 3:** extra time is normally 25%, more if needed. **(Not verified):** how extra time is applied within the oral.
- **Recommendation (UX-OQ-33):**
  - an opt-in "**practice with extra time**" setting (+25% and +50%) that applies to the look, answer and grace times
  - never call it an "adjusted exam", "approved" or "official"
  - tag those attempts in history
  - an info sheet explaining the real application process (the winter 2026 deadline has passed; ask the school about May–June 2027)
  - store no reason or diagnosis
