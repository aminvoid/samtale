## TTS (lexicon additions)
- **New rules:** CV'et → *se ve et* (placed before CV), CV → *se ve*, CO2 → *ce o to*, B12 → *be tolv*, RKI → *er ka i*, DR → *de er*, `10-15 timer` → *ti til femten timer*.
- **Fixes to existing rules:** SU'en → *es u en*, SU-lånet → *es u lånet*.
- **Listening test:** „bedst før“-datoen, *Too Good To Go*, *image*, `social- og sundhedsassistenter` (now also in 2022-nov-a and 2023-nov-b), bare numbers, A-/B-hold, loanwords.

## Ideas for teaching
- prepositions in set phrases (*lugte til*, *smage på*, *føle sig udenfor*)
- which verbs take *godt* (*det tror jeg godt* but *det synes jeg*)
- subject or object after V2
- collocations (*løse et problem* but *fjerne en årsag*; *et godt helbred*)
