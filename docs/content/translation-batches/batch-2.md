## Uncertain items

| Item ID | Reason and draft reading |
|---|---|
| `2018-maj-a.general.q2.answer` | *spare på personalet* leaves the form of the staffing savings open. The draft refers to economizing on staffing, which can include fewer workers or fewer hours. |
| `2018-maj-b.sitA.example1` | *På et hospital skal man blive hjemme* leaves the actor and the condition implicit. The draft reads *på et hospital* as working at a hospital. The surrounding answer supplies the illness context, which the draft leaves implicit. |
| `2018-nov-b.sitA.q1.answer` | In *lære et nyt sprog og en ny kultur at kende*, *at kende* can attach to both objects. The draft reads it as learning a new language and getting to know a new culture. |
| `2018-nov-b.sitB.q1.answer` | *afklaring* leaves open how far the young people have decided what to study. The draft uses «روشن شدن مسیر». |
| `2018-nov-b.sitB.followup1.answer` | *når de starter* leaves the activity unstated. The context suggests starting a course of study, and the draft leaves the activity implicit. |
| `2018-nov-c.sitA.followup1.answer` | *fejle* can mean making mistakes or failing more broadly. The draft uses «از اشتباه کردن بترسند» in the context of young children and testing. |
| `2019-nov-a.general.q2.answer` | *i fagene* can refer to fields of study or occupations, and *kommer ind* leaves the destination unstated. The draft uses «رشته‌ها» and leaves the place of admission implicit. |
