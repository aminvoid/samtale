# Accessibility audit (WCAG 2.2 AA): the design system and the first pages

- **Auditor:** `accessibility-auditor`, 2026-11-20/21 (read-only; the main thread saved this condensed record).
- **Tools:** Playwright 1.65.1 (Chromium and WebKit), axe 4.14 (WCAG 2.2 tags), light and dark themes, 375 and 1280 px, keyboard, zoom up to 400%, 1.4.12 text spacing, reduced motion and forced colours. Contrast was recomputed from `src/styles/tokens.css`, and all published ratios match.
- **Not tested:** screen readers (VoiceOver and TalkBack in Persian) need a human; the script is at the end. The mock exam screens, dialogs, audio player and recorder were audited as **specs only**. The new `src/components/ui/` components were out of scope.

## Conformance
- **Running pages** (`/fa`, the Persian 404, `/fa/test-error`): **NOT DETERMINED.** No A/AA failure was found, but the pages are minimal and no screen-reader test has been done.
- **The design system and mock exam specs as written:** **DOES NOT CONFORM if built as specified.** S1–S4 must be fixed in the specs before the mock exam is built.

## Serious
- **S1. The audio scrubber reverses in RTL** (2.1.1, 4.1.2). Base UI's Slider reads direction from React context, not from the DOM `dir`.
  - **Fix:** wrap the player in `<DirectionProvider direction="ltr"><div dir="ltr">`, and keep SpeedControl outside it.
  - **Test:** ArrowRight increases `aria-valuenow`.
- **S2. Sticky offsets don't match the bar heights** (2.4.11). The mock header is 88 px vs a 56 px offset; the answer area is about 300 px vs a 144 px offset.
  - **Fix:** measure the `[data-sticky]` heights with a ResizeObserver and set the CSS variables, using 0 when a bar isn't pinned.
- **S3. The pinned zones break at 200% zoom and in landscape** (1.4.10, 2.4.11): 388 of 400 px are fixed.
  - **Fix:** unpin both bars on short viewports (`max-height: 30rem`, or when the area is more than 40% of the viewport height). Make this condition mandatory in DF-10.
- **S4. "Current" indicators rely on hue or very faint fills** (1.4.1 A, 1.4.11). The tab bar uses colour only (1.03:1), the pills are 1.17:1 and the segments 1.15:1.
  - **Fix:** add a ≥ 3:1 shape cue: an accent bar or border, or a check icon.

## Moderate
- **M1.** Danish inside `aria-label` strings or live-region text loses its `lang`. Take names from content (`<span lang="da">`), and render markup in the live region.
- **M2.** RepeatControl: use `aria-labelledby` for the action label and `aria-describedby` for the Danish and Persian lines, so nothing is read twice.
- **M3.** Toggle buttons: keep a constant name with `aria-pressed`; don't change the label as well.
- **M4.** Collapsible blocks: put the button inside the `h3` (APG accordion pattern), not the heading inside the button.
- **M5.** Compact SituationCard: wrap the text instead of using an ellipsis plus `title`.
- **M6.** The infinite `rec-pulse` and `speaking` animations: stop them after 5 s or less (e.g. 3 iterations).
- **M7.** Exam conditions:
  - say up front that answers end at the limit
  - **always** show and announce the handover and grace notices, whatever the timer setting (closes UX-OQ-03)
  - make the one-minute notice independent of timer visibility
- **M8.** Screen-reader users in exam conditions:
  - add a headphones tip
  - queue in-answer announcements after Done (keep only the grace and handover notices live)
  - consider an "adjusted exam" option recorded on the attempt; ask `pd3-exam-expert` about special arrangements in PD3 first
- **M9.** Toasts are for redundant confirmations only. Errors (for example "couldn't save") are persistent notices.
- **M10.** Shadowing SentenceText: each sentence is a real `<button>`.
- **M11.** Forced colours: selected and current states need a border or outline (`Highlight`).

## Minor
- **m1.** The 404 doesn't move focus to its `h1`, and doesn't use the title template.
- **m2.** The error page's `document.title` doesn't describe the error.
- **m3.** The 404 home link is below the 44 px target.
- **m4.** The error page needs a "back home" action, and the retry button looks like a link.
- **m5.** SpeedControl radios need names such as «سرعت ۰٫۷۵ برابر».
- **m6.** Switch off thumb contrast is 1.15:1; use `bg-text-muted`.
- **m7.** Arc and fill vs track is 2.01:1. Document it as a conscious choice, or lighten the track.
- **m8.** Drawn-card thumbnails: make them `disabled`, or remove them from the tab order.
- **m9.** The LiveRegion needs an explicit `aria-live="polite"`. Base UI's modal hides `role="status"`-only regions.
- **m10.** Preview-page markup issues (internal; not shipped).
- **m11.** Tests to add: axe in dark mode, the skip-link flow, a visible-focus check and the S1 ArrowRight test.

## Working well
- Contrast is solid. The lowest text pair is 5.31:1.
- The focus ring is an outline and survives forced colours. The skip link is the first Tab stop and moves focus to `main`.
- `lang` and `dir` are correct. Danish runs read left to right inside RTL in both engines.
- No overflow at 320 px, 200% or 400% zoom, or with the 1.4.12 overrides. Reduced motion is respected.
- The timing design is careful: no countdown except the look, no colour escalation, one toggle hides all timers, and technical interruptions freeze time.
- The announcement plan, the persistent primary-action slot, and roles signalled by icon, label and colour (never colour alone).

## Screen-reader script (for a human)
Run on VoiceOver (iOS Safari, macOS Safari) and TalkBack (Android Chrome with a Persian TTS engine).
1. `/fa`: the title is announced, and the skip link is first and works.
2. Heading navigation finds one `h1`. How is "PD3" read?
3. Danish runs: is the Danish voice used? (decides M1)
4. 404: title and home link.
5. Error page: focus on the heading, then the retry button.
6. Mock exam: is the grace announcement heard while the Leave dialog is open? (m9)
7. Draw cards: "card 1 of 3"; the reveal announcement and which voice reads it.
8. Answer phase on the speaker: does screen-reader speech get into the recording? Is the 10 s look feasible at all? (M8)
9. Scrubber: does swiping up move forward, and is the value read as «۰:۱۲ از ۰:۳۱»? (S1)
10. RepeatControl: the name is the action only, with the Danish read once as the hint. (M2)
